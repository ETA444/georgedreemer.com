My BA at Hampshire College was a four-year, self-designed program focused on marketing, business, and entrepreneurship.

The curriculum connected marketing and consumer behaviour, business fundamentals, and persuasive communication: learning to research markets, assess business opportunities, negotiate agreements, and develop the strategy and visual identity behind a venture.

Through the Five College Consortium, I took accounting at Smith College and graduate-level coursework in Internet Business, Business Law, and Negotiation Theory & Practice at UMass Amherst's Isenberg School of Management. Alongside coursework and independent studies, I built the streetwear brand SkipSchoolMakeMoney and served on Hampshire's student-run Seed Fund for Innovation, evaluating venture pitches and voting on funding decisions.
